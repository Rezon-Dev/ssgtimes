Our Standards · Read Before You Submit

Satire with a purpose. Stories with responsibility.

Humour gives us room to question the ordinary. Responsibility tells us where to draw the line. This policy explains how SSG TIMES approaches stories, submissions, privacy, corrections and satire.

Applies to editorial content published on SSG TIMES · Unofficial publication
01 / PURPOSE

What we are here to do

SSG TIMES is an unofficial, student-oriented satirical publication focused on school-life humour, creative writing, observations, opinions, cartoons and nostalgia.

Our purpose is to entertain, encourage creativity and present familiar experiences from an imaginative perspective. We do not claim to be an official school newspaper or an authorised source of school announcements.

02 / SATIRE & FICTION

Make the joke clear

Satire may exaggerate routines, situations and everyday frustrations for comic effect. Some articles, characters, dialogue and scenarios may be fictional or fictionalised.

  • Readers should be able to distinguish humorous commentary from verified reporting.
  • Fictional scenes must not be presented as proven real events.
  • Humour should not become targeted humiliation, harassment or bullying.
  • We may revise wording or add clarification when a joke could reasonably be mistaken for a factual accusation.
03 / ACCURACY & EVIDENCE

Claims need care

A satirical tone does not remove the responsibility to treat factual claims carefully. Where an article makes a meaningful factual assertion, we aim to distinguish known information, personal opinion, interpretation and invented material.

  • We do not knowingly publish fabricated claims as established facts.
  • Submissions may be checked, edited, delayed or rejected when details cannot be reasonably assessed.
  • Unverified accusations about identifiable people are not suitable for publication.
  • Publication should not be interpreted as proof that every detail has been independently verified.
04 / PRIVACY & SAFETY

Some things should stay private

Stories should not expose someone's private life simply to make content more dramatic. We prioritise dignity and safety, especially in a school community where many people are minors.

  • Do not submit private chats, passwords, phone numbers, home addresses or personal documents.
  • Do not publish confidential school records, private medical details or sensitive personal information.
  • Do not submit content intended to threaten, shame, harass or identify a person for ridicule.
  • Use consent and care when submitting identifiable photographs, quotations or personal stories.

We may anonymise, edit or decline material when necessary to reduce privacy or safety risks. Do not send sensitive information expecting it to be published.

05 / READER SUBMISSIONS

Original work, honestly submitted

We welcome original stories, satire, opinions, memories and cartoon concepts. Submissions should be respectful, relevant to the publication and suitable for a school-age audience.

  • Submit work you created or have permission to share.
  • Credit sources and collaborators when appropriate.
  • Do not submit plagiarism, impersonation or content copied without permission.
  • Editors may make changes for clarity, length, grammar, style or safety.
  • Sending a submission does not guarantee acceptance or publication.

Unless separately agreed, submitting work does not mean that SSG TIMES endorses every view expressed in it. Contributors should retain their own copies of their work.

06 / OPINION & COMMENTARY

Disagreement is allowed

Opinions may challenge ideas, routines and common assumptions. Strong criticism can be useful, but it should focus on the issue rather than attack a person's identity, private life or dignity.

An opinion article represents the perspective expressed by its author and should not automatically be read as the position of every contributor or of the school.

07 / EDITING & PUBLICATION

Not every story makes the front page

Editorial decisions may consider relevance, originality, clarity, tone, accuracy, privacy, safety and available space. We may decline a submission without publishing it or providing a detailed explanation.

We may edit titles, grammar, formatting and length while taking reasonable care not to distort the author's intended meaning. Content may be withheld or removed when concerns arise after publication.

08 / CORRECTIONS & CONCERNS

When something needs fixing

If you believe an article contains a material error, exposes private information or could reasonably be mistaken for an unverified factual allegation, contact the editorial inbox with the page URL and a clear explanation.

We may review the concern and, where appropriate, correct, clarify, update or remove the material. Contacting us does not guarantee a particular outcome, but specific and relevant details help us assess the issue.

09 / INDEPENDENCE & AFFILIATION

Unofficial means unofficial

SSG TIMES is an independent creative project and is not an official communication channel for Sainik School Goalpara. Its articles should not be treated as official notices, school policy, administrative statements or verified institutional reporting.

Names, settings or familiar school-life references may appear for commentary, humour or context. Their appearance does not imply endorsement, sponsorship or approval by the school.

10 / POLICY UPDATES

A living editorial document

These standards may be updated as the website develops and new editorial needs arise. Visitors should consult this page for the current version.

This policy describes the publication's intended editorial practices. It is not a guarantee that every submission or published page will be error-free.

The rule above every rule

Be funny without being cruel. Be critical without inventing allegations. Tell stories without violating someone's privacy. The best satire leaves readers thinking—not someone fearing what will be exposed next.
