Editor's Welcome

Welcome to SSG Times

Where campus life meets curiosity, ordinary incidents become extraordinary headlines, and every day brings a new story worth telling. 🔎

SSG Times Desk · Front Page

✏️ 📰 Front-page illustration Add your image as assets/images/front-page-doodle.webp.

Behind every uniform lies a story. Behind every classroom, a thousand untold incidents. And behind every perfectly ordinary school day? Well, there's probably something ridiculous happening that deserves its own headline. 😂

From hostel mysteries 🏫 and classroom comedy 📚 to legendary cadet moments 🏆, old memories 📸, campus observations, and the wonderfully unpredictable life at SSG, we're here to capture the side of school life that rarely makes it into official reports.

We're not here to write another school brochure praising every brick, tree, and timetable. We're here for the stories, the humour, the nostalgia, the chaos, and the little truths that make campus life unforgettable. 🎭

“Bhai, yeh toh bilkul sach hai!” 😂

Why SSG Times?

Every good newspaper needs a little drama. 🕵️ But we believe in keeping the humour playful, the stories responsible, and the facts separate from the fiction.

Because schools have official histories, but students have stories of their own. ✨
