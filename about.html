Our Story

A newspaper for the stories hiding between the bells.

Every school has two versions of its daily life: the one written in official notices, timetables and neatly arranged schedules, and the one experienced by the people actually living through it. SSG TIMES is interested in the second one.

We look at the everyday theatre of campus life: the mysterious disappearance of free time, the legendary power of an announcement, the complicated relationship between cadets and homework, and the small moments that somehow become permanent memories.

Our approach is simple. Take familiar situations, observe them closely, add a little imagination, and present them with the seriousness of a national inquiry into a missing lunch break.

“The bell rings. The day continues. The investigation begins.” The unofficial newsroom philosophy

SSG TIMES is not an official school newspaper, administrative channel or representative of the school. It is an independent creative project built around humour, storytelling, student observations and satire.

Some stories may be fictional, exaggerated or inspired by common school experiences. The goal is to make readers smile, think, remember and occasionally wonder why a perfectly ordinary event required a full investigation.

The Editorial Code

Three rules of the newsroom.

01 / HUMOUR

Laugh, don't harm.

Satire should be clever rather than cruel. We aim at familiar situations and absurdities, not at humiliating individuals.

02 / RESPONSIBILITY

Privacy matters.

Do not submit private messages, confidential documents, identifying personal details or unverified accusations. Submissions may be edited or declined.

03 / CREATIVITY

Bring your own voice.

Original stories, sharp observations, thoughtful opinions and imaginative cartoons are welcome. Good writing matters more than dramatic claims.
