Before Sending

Submission Checklist

  • Use an original title.
  • Check spelling and readability.
  • Separate facts from opinions and satire.
  • Get permission for artwork you did not create.
  • Leave out passwords, private messages, and personal details.
  • Do not make unsupported allegations about identifiable people.
