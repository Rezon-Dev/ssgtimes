Case No. 001 · The Big Picture

505 Acres of Land, 600 Cadets, and Not a Single Guarantee of Eight Hours of Sleep

SSG TIMES BUREAU · CAMPUS LIFE DESK

The campus has space for ambitious dreams, long marches, academic targets, and approximately six hundred different interpretations of what it means to be tired.

Then comes the familiar advice: teenagers need sufficient sleep. The recommendation is clear. The timetable, meanwhile, continues operating with the confidence of an institution that has never personally needed a nap.

Our imaginary investigators have reviewed the evidence: early mornings, classes, activities, assignments, and the mysterious ability of unfinished work to appear just before bedtime.

Preliminary finding

The importance of sleep is well documented. The mystery is how students are expected to fit enough of it into a demanding day.

Case No. 002 · Health Desk

The MI Room: Please Register Your Illness

THE ADMINISTRATIVE ADVENTURE

Every great mystery begins with a question. Sometimes it begins with a register, a pen, and a person attempting to explain why they feel unwell.

Meanwhile, health talks remind students about hygiene and sleep. Sensible advice—followed by the bureau's most difficult question: how does the recommended bedtime fit into a busy timetable?

Official finding

Health matters. So does making time to look after it.

Case No. 003 · Social Structure

The Real Government of SSG: The Unofficial Edition

THE DEPARTMENT OF WHO TOLD YOU THAT?

A simple instruction leaves one person. It passes through several others. By the time it reaches its destination, it may have gained three new conditions and an imaginary official seal.

Leadership is responsibility, not a royal title. Respect works better when it travels in both directions.
